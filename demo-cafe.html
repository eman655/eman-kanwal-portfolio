Welcome

About Cafe & Co

Cafe & Co is Rahim Yar Khan's cosy spot on East Canal Road — where friends meet over coffee, families share a karahi, and every plate is made fresh to order. From crispy zingers to sizzling BBQ platters, our kitchen keeps it simple: good ingredients, honest prices and service with a smile.
